Site4Site is a personal digital library where you can save useful websites,
manage everyday tasks, and keep track of anime, movies, and games—all in one
place.

## Features

### 🔗 Site4Site

Organize your favorite and frequently used websites.

- Create separate sections for topics such as work, study, or entertainment.
- Add websites with a name, URL, and personal note.
- Edit section and website information.
- Delete websites or entire sections you no longer need.
- Open saved websites directly from your library.

### ✅ Site4Tasks

Keep track of simple everyday tasks.

- Quickly add new tasks.
- Mark tasks as completed or incomplete.
- Edit existing tasks.
- Delete tasks when they are no longer needed.

### 🎬 Site4Anime

Build and manage your personal anime collection.

- Search for anime by title.
- View information and genres for each anime.
- Add personal notes.
- Organize anime into:
  - Watching
  - Plan to Watch
  - Completed
- Update or remove saved anime at any time.

### 🍿 Site4Movies

Save movies you have watched or want to watch later.

- Search for movies and view their details.
- Add personal notes to saved movies.
- Mark movies as:
  - Watched
  - Plan to Watch
- Filter your movie collection by status.
- Update or delete saved movies.

### 🎮 Site4Games

Create a collection of games you are interested in.

- Search for games and explore their details.
- View available trailers and screenshots.
- Write a personal review or note.
- Track games as:
  - Playing
  - Completed
  - Dropped
  - Wishlist
- Update or remove games from your collection.

## Additional Features

- **Passwordless sign-in** — securely access your account through an email magic
  link without remembering a password.
- **Personal library** — saved content is connected to your account.
- **Quick navigation** — move between sites, tasks, anime, movies, and games
  using the sidebar.
- **Dark and light themes** — choose the appearance that suits you.
- **Responsive design** — use the site across desktop and mobile screen sizes.
- **Instant feedback** — receive clear notifications when content is added,
  updated, or deleted.

## How It Works

1. Sign in using your email address.
2. Open a section from the sidebar.
3. Search for entertainment or add your own websites and tasks.
4. Save items with a status and personal notes.
5. Return later to update and manage your collection.

Site4Site keeps the things you care about organized and makes them easy to find
when you need them.
